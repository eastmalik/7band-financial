# 7Band Financial Agency — Design Brief

## Reference
The site closely follows the layout and structure of buildalifeloc.com: dark hero, clean white content sections, green accent labels, orange CTAs, 3-column feature cards, process diagram, about section, final CTA, and footer.

## Three Stylistic Approaches

### Option A — "Generational Vault" (Probability: 0.07)
Dark navy + gold. Serious, legacy-wealth aesthetic. Heavy serif display type, gold accents, textured dark backgrounds. Feels like a private bank.

### Option B — "Modern Protection" (Probability: 0.04)
Clean white with deep forest green and warm amber. Approachable, trustworthy, community-oriented. Geometric shapes, clean sans-serif, editorial layout.

### Option C — "7Band Bold" (Probability: 0.09)
Deep midnight navy hero, crisp white content sections, electric green accent labels, warm orange CTA buttons — directly mirrors the buildalifeloc.com reference. Professional, action-oriented, and immediately familiar to the client.

---

## Chosen Approach: **7Band Bold** (Option C)

### Design Movement
Contemporary financial services editorial — clean, authoritative, and conversion-focused. Mirrors the reference site's proven layout while elevating it with stronger brand identity for 7Band Financial Agency.

### Core Principles
1. Dark hero with strong headline contrast — commands attention immediately
2. Clean white content sections for readability and trust
3. Orange CTAs that demand action — never passive
4. Green accent labels that organize content hierarchy

### Color Philosophy
- **Hero background**: `#0d1f2d` — deep midnight navy, conveys security and stability
- **Accent green**: `#22c55e` — vibrant, life-affirming, signals growth
- **CTA orange**: `#f97316` — urgency, warmth, action
- **Body text**: `#1e293b` on white — authoritative, readable
- **White sections**: `#ffffff` — clean, trustworthy, open

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric hero (text left, visual right), then alternating full-width white content sections. 3-column card grid for feature highlights. Process flow diagram. Split bio section (text left, image right).

### Signature Elements
1. Shield/crest logo mark — protection symbolism
2. Green pill/badge labels above section headings
3. Orange filled primary button + dark outlined secondary button pairing

### Interaction Philosophy
Smooth scroll, subtle entrance animations on section reveal, button press scale effect. Nothing distracting — the content is the hero.

### Animation
- Sections fade up on scroll (opacity 0→1, translateY 20px→0, 400ms ease-out)
- Buttons scale to 0.97 on active press
- Nav transitions to opaque white on scroll past hero

### Typography System
- Display/headings: **Playfair Display** — authoritative, legacy feel
- Body/UI: **Inter** — clean, modern, readable
- Hero headline: 3.5rem bold Playfair Display
- Section headings: 2rem bold Playfair Display
- Body: 1rem/1.6 Inter regular

### Brand Essence
**7Band Financial Agency** — Life insurance and generational wealth for families who are serious about their legacy. Trustworthy. Bold. Generational.

### Brand Voice
Direct, empowering, no jargon. Headlines: "Protect Your Family. Build Their Future." CTA: "Book Your Free Consultation." Ban: "Welcome to our website", "Get started today".

### Wordmark & Logo
Bold "7" numeral inside a shield/crest mark. No decorative font — the mark IS the brand.

### Signature Brand Color
Electric green `#22c55e` — the color of growth, life, and financial protection.

## Style Decisions
- Hero uses dark navy `#0d1f2d` background with white headline text
- All section label badges use green background with white text
- Primary CTA always orange; secondary CTA always dark outlined
- Playfair Display for all headings, Inter for all body copy

## Dual-Mode Experience — Chosen Direction

### Design Movement
**Dual-Realm Financial Experience.** The website intentionally supports two ways to receive the same strategy: a calm, conventional editorial experience for visitors who want direct clarity and an immersive Player 1 interface for visitors who want the full 7Band world.

### Core Principles
1. **Same truth, different entry point.** The agency, services, educational disclosures, and Calendly call to action stay consistent in both views.
2. **Choice without judgment.** The public-facing control uses **Simple View** and **Player 1** so the visitor chooses their preferred level of immersion without being labeled.
3. **One deliberate transformation.** A single persistent switch morphs the complete route, not merely its colors or one isolated section.
4. **Clarity before spectacle.** Simple View prioritizes readable financial education, while Player 1 retains the game vocabulary for those who choose it.

### Simple View Color Philosophy
Simple View is a crisp financial-editorial system: deep navy (`#0B1F3A`) establishes trust, mineral blue (`#2563EB`) signals momentum and financial clarity, soft sky (`#EAF3FF`) creates breathing room, and warm brass (`#B8892E`) preserves a quiet connection to the 7Band mark. It is light, welcoming, and unmistakably professional without becoming generic.

### Simple View Layout Paradigm
An editorial path replaces the HUD: an asymmetric hero with an information panel, a clear two-service split, alternating white and pale-blue bands, and a sticky-but-unobtrusive navigation bar. Cards use restrained borders, generous white space, a left-aligned hierarchy, and minimal rounding.

### Signature Elements
1. A slender **View Mode** switch with a blue active track for Simple View and a gold active track for Player 1.
2. A vertical brass rule or small seven-point compass motif used as an editorial marker rather than game chrome.
3. Layered paper-blue field patterns and fine blueprint lines in Simple View.

### Interaction and Animation
Switching views uses a 220ms opacity-and-transform transition with an accessible live label. The selected view persists locally. Simple View uses short, quiet hover and reveal motion; Player 1 keeps the existing HUD glows and quest-card behavior. All nonessential motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Simple View pairs **Cinzel** for decisive display headings with **Rajdhani** for labels and a calm, highly legible system sans-serif for body content. Player 1 preserves the existing Cinzel/Rajdhani hierarchy. The change is treatment and spacing rather than a confused font swap.

### Brand Voice by Mode
Simple View is plainspoken: “Build stronger credit. Explore life insurance strategies. Protect what matters.” Player 1 remains cinematic: “Learn the rules. Build your system. Fund your future.” Both modes avoid guarantees, jargon-heavy claims, and artificial urgency.
